We automatically screen our KOL roster against Binance's affiliate program requirements. Verified KOLs get fast-tracked.

Binance KOL Requirements

To qualify for the Binance KOL/Affiliate program, influencers must meet at least one of the following criteria:

  • ✓5,000+ followers on social media (Twitter, YouTube, etc.)
  • ✓500+ members in community groups (Telegram, Discord)
  • ✓Active crypto content creation history
  • ✓41~50% spot commission + 30~40% futures commission
  • ✓Quarterly performance review (10+ traders, 50+ BTC volume)
